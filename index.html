Minecraft skin checks before upload

Check these three things before exporting a player skin.

Match the arm model

Pick classic or slim in your editor and use the same model when you import the skin. The two templates wrap sleeves differently.

Inspect the seams

Rotate the preview to check the front, back and inside of both arms. Toggle the outer layer to make sure the base texture still holds up.

Test the exported PNG

Reload the saved PNG in your editor or in the game. Keep a separate copy of the original before making changes.
